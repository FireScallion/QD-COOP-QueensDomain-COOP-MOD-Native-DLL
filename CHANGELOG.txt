QD COOP 1.18.0-preview3 - Co-op progression and room recovery

Guest saves retain shared world progress and the character's own safe position
when subsequently hosting. Personal inventory, XP, dialogue and world pickups
remain personal, including pickups in unloaded areas.
Repair a missing personal Stone Duck after the native first-boss reward claim;
respect an already owned duck or completed shop unlock, with a saved receipt.
Accept native loot tables' default -1 count; stop treasure-lizard replica death
from executing an extra independent drop. Native personal loot rolls remain.
Preserve treasure-lizard flee targets until native fear decays.
Aggro cone is 120 degrees; 1.5x recent damage takes priority, with existing decay
and bounded attack commitment. No sampling-rate or player-animation changes.
Follow the host's actual music cue through the original music controller.
Replay lever motion/audio, dagger-button motion/sparks/audio, and gargoyle
ignition presentation without replaying gameplay activation/reward operations.
Release departed network peers promptly; reopen Steam joining after departure,
retry presence publication, and recover an exited Steam helper without restarting
the game. Existing LAN rooms remain independent of helper recovery.
This is still the two-player UE4SS branch; the native DLL four-player branch is separate.

修复客机保存共同进度后转为房主仍回到开场的问题，保留个人角色与独立拾取。
补发已领取但客机漏收的首 Boss 石鸭；已持有或已解锁商店时不重复发放。
修复常规掉落记录被错误丢弃，以及星蜥死亡演出额外掉落、逃跑被强制中断。
仇恨视角调整为 120°，另一玩家达到当前目标 1.5 倍近期有效伤害时优先转移。
修复 Boss 结束与场景变化后的音乐跟随，补充拉杆、飞刀按钮和石像鬼机关演出。
修复离房后席位释放、Steam 加入重新开放、后台异常退出后的自动恢复。
双方请更新到同一版本。本次测试使用隔离副本，保留原安装与存档。

QD COOP 1.18.0-preview2 - Player appearances preview

Added five persistent, selectable appearances to the right of the Multiplayer menu.
Arjaot, Ippibi and Ujji use helmet 00; Imba uses closed helmet 01.
Selection UI follows Chinese/English language selection and uses matching
neutral arms-down portraits, with slightly opened elbows for armour clearance.
Bound the soldier meshes offline to the existing prince skeleton. Canonicalized
FBX bind transforms so the existing native full-body graph can drive them.
No additional live animation driver, NPC AI, preview capture, or sampling changes.
Cosmetic state has its own optional Y|1 tag; save-slot S semantics are unchanged.
Original mods and saves are retained. Steam preview limitations remain unchanged.

新增五种可记忆外观、多人菜单右侧预览和中英文选择按钮。
士兵模型离线适配现有王子骨架和全身动作，修正 FBX 导入轴向差异；
预览站姿的手肘略微外展，避免贴入盔甲。
